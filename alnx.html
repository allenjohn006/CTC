Who Can Join?

If you are eligible to donate blood, come prepared to take part. Volunteers are also welcome to support registration and coordination on the day of the event.

Contact for Registration

Why Donate?

  • It helps accident victims, surgical patients, and emergency cases.
  • One donation can support multiple patients.
  • It is a direct act of service to the community.

Before You Come

  • Eat a healthy meal and stay hydrated.
  • Carry a valid ID if required by organizers.
  • Get enough rest before donation day.

Volunteering

  • Help guide donors and manage the registration desk.
  • Support the awareness and awareness-sharing activities.
  • Be part of the NSS team effort from start to finish.
Be present on 1st September at El Shaddhai Auditorium. Bring your friends, spread the message, and help make the campaign a success.
